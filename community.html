Community & contributing

Link with iQ is built in the open. Anyone can file an issue, pick one up, or point us at a problem worth solving — no invitation needed.

Where to start

Three ways in

01 / GRAB AN ISSUE

Pick up open work

Browse issues tagged "good first issue" below, or dig through any repo's open issues directly.

02 / SUGGEST A PROBLEM

Tell us what's missing

Have a real problem you think fits what we build? Send it our way — most of our projects started as someone else's suggestion.

03 / JUST SHIP SOMETHING

Fork it and go

Everything's MIT-licensed and open. Fork a repo, build on it, ship your own version — you don't need our permission.

    How discussion works

    Comments & discussion

    We're setting up GitHub Discussions on our repositories so conversation about a project can live right next to its code — no separate forum account needed. Once that's connected, you'll be able to comment on blog posts and project updates directly with your GitHub login.

    In the meantime, the fastest way to talk to us is to open an issue or send an email — we read everything.
