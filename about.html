Link with iQ is an independent research and development organization. We research ideas, develop useful technologies, and create practical solutions to real-world problems — aiming to make technology more useful, private, accessible, simple, open, and beneficial to people.

What we believe

Technology should have a purpose

We don't believe technology should be created simply because something is technically possible. Technology should not exist only for complexity, profit, or convenience for a small number of people. Good technology should solve meaningful problems, respect people and their privacy, and be accessible to as many people as possible.

Our mission

Practical technology, built responsibly

To research, develop, and share practical technology that solves real-world problems while prioritizing privacy, accessibility, simplicity, openness, and human benefit. We aim to turn ideas and research into useful tools, software, products, and resources that can make people's lives easier and technology better.

Our principles

What we don't compromise on

01

Privacy First

We aim to minimize unnecessary data collection, reduce unnecessary tracking, and give users meaningful control over their information. Whenever technically practical, we prefer approaches that allow data to remain under the user's control. Privacy should be considered during the design of a technology, not added after it is finished.

02

Open by Default

Technology and knowledge become more valuable when people can inspect, understand, learn from, and improve them. Our goal is: open as much as responsibly possible. Some projects may remain partially or completely closed when security, privacy, legal, safety, or third-party licensing considerations require it.

03

Free & Accessible

We want useful technology available to as many people as possible. Where practical, we aim to provide tools and resources free of charge — particularly for students, developers, researchers, communities, and people who may not have access to expensive technology.

04

Simplicity

Technology should not be unnecessarily complicated. We aim to remove unnecessary complexity and create tools that are understandable and practical. A powerful tool should still be easy to use.

05

Real-World Impact

We focus on problems rather than technology for its own sake: Problem → Research → Experiment → Prototype → Build → Improve. A project doesn't need to be revolutionary to be valuable — solving a small problem effectively can make a meaningful difference.

06

Continuous Improvement

Technology is constantly changing. We experiment, test, learn from failures, improve our designs, and keep developing. A failed experiment can still produce valuable knowledge.

Our approach to open source

Open, and responsibly so

Where appropriate, we aim to publish source code, libraries, tools, documentation, research, technical explanations, experiments, specifications, and educational resources. We encourage developers, researchers, students, and communities to learn from our work and, where the applicable license permits, contribute to or build upon it.

However, openness must also be responsible. Projects involving sensitive security research, privacy risks, third-party restrictions, or other legitimate concerns may require limitations on what can be publicly released.

Our vision

An ecosystem worth building

Our long-term vision is to build an ecosystem of useful technologies, research, tools, and resources that people can access, understand, and benefit from. We want Link with iQ to become a place where problems are researched, ideas are explored, experiments are encouraged, useful tools are built, and knowledge is shared — where open-source projects grow, privacy is respected, technology becomes easier to use, developers can build upon existing work, and new ideas can become practical solutions.
