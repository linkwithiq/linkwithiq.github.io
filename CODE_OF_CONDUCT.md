# Code of Conduct

Link with iQ is built in the open, and we want taking part to be safe and welcoming for everyone, whatever their background, experience, or ability.

## What we expect
- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information without their consent.
- Keep contributions in line with the project's principles: no tracking, no fabricated claims (see the [Policy page](https://linkwithiq.github.io/policy.html)).

## What we won't accept
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome attention.
- Spam, or posting other people's private information.

## Scope
This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where people represent or take part in Link with iQ.

## Reporting and enforcement
Email **linkwithiq@proton.me** with what happened and where. Reports are handled privately by the maintainer (Ajit). Depending on the situation, we may ask someone to change their behaviour, remove comments or contributions, or block them from the project.

## Attribution
Written in plain language, informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
