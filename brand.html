Brand kit

The name, the mark, the colours, and the type: everything you need to write about or link to Link with iQ accurately.

The name

Write it Link with iQ: capital L, lowercase "with", lowercase i, capital Q. Not "LinkWithIQ" or "Link With IQ". The short form is "Link with iQ" too, and there's no separate acronym.

Typography

Oswald (uppercase, headings), Inter (body text), and JetBrains Mono (labels and code). All three are open fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and the files are served from this site's assets/fonts folder.

Full

Link with iQ (linkwithiq.github.io) is an independent research and development organization researching, building, and sharing practical technology that is private, accessible, simple, and open. Every project and resource is free to use, and the code is open so anyone can inspect, fork, or build on it. The site uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking, and says plainly what is finished and what is still an idea. Link with iQ is an early-stage independent project, not a registered company. It is founded, owned, and maintained by Ajit (asrbmy).
