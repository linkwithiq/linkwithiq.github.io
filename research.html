How we approach it

Research, not just requirements-gathering

We're not trying to publish academic papers — this is closer to a public lab notebook. Before committing to an approach, we try to understand the existing landscape: what's been tried, what similar tools exist, and why people aren't already using them. Publishing that groundwork means the reasoning behind a project's shape is visible, not just the finished result.

Know something we don't?

Point us at what we're missing

If you've researched a problem space we're working in — or you know prior art we should be looking at — we'd genuinely like to hear about it.
