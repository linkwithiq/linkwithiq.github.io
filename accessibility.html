Accessibility

Where this site meets accessibility standards, where it doesn't, and how to tell us when something gets in your way.

Where we stand

We aim for WCAG 2.2 Level AA and, as far as we've checked, this site meets it, and WCAG 2.1 AA too. We do not claim Level AAA. We've closed some AAA gaps (text contrast of at least 7:1 on every dark surface, and 44×44px targets on the main controls), but AAA also covers things like reading level that we haven't assessed. Our wider commitments are on the Policy page.

What the site does

  • A skip-to-content link on every page, and full keyboard operation of the menu, filters, and FAQ.
  • Visible focus outlines, and anchor links that don't land underneath the sticky header.
  • Text and interface colours checked against contrast thresholds computed from the design tokens.
  • Motion that respects prefers-reduced-motion, and a loading screen that always finishes on its own.
  • Support for forced-colors (high-contrast) mode and a print stylesheet.
  • Real headings, landmarks, and link text, and no information carried by colour alone.
  • Fonts served from this site, resizable with your browser's zoom and text-size settings.

How we've tested

Contrast ratios were calculated from the colour values in styles.css. Automated checks run on every change to catch broken links, anchors, and invalid markup. Keyboard behaviour and focus handling were reviewed by hand. We have not had an external accessibility audit, and we haven't tested with every screen reader and browser combination, so there may be problems we haven't found.

Known limits

  • Level AAA is not met overall (see above).
  • Live GitHub content, such as issue titles and contributor names, comes from third parties, and we don't control its wording or structure.
  • Placeholder pages (like the Now page) are hidden from search until they have real content.

Report a barrier

If something on this site is hard or impossible for you to use, email linkwithiq@proton.me with the page, what you were trying to do, and (if you're comfortable sharing) the browser or assistive technology you use. We're a small team and can't promise a fixed response time, but we read every report and fix what we can. You can also open an issue on GitHub.
